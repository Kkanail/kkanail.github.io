Light

Depth = how far the light falls after the water bends it. Deeper makes sharper, tangled lines. Grain 0 lines the particles up in rows (fine stripes); 1 scatters them like film grain. Afterglow blends each frame with the last: smoother, dreamier.

Water

Calm = how fast ripples fade. Swell = slow waves underneath, so the light never stops moving. Walls bounce ripples back from the edges.
