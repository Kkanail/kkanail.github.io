Kailan Huang/ Playground/

Turner Underwater

Tap the painting to shatter it

Painting

Three paintings, three scenes. On the floor: the painting lies at the bottom of the pool and the light webs play over it. As particles: the painting is made of glowing dots, and the water bends each one; tap the painting and it shatters from that spot, then the dots gather into the next picture. Focus 0 leaves them in chaos.

Score

Bursts land on the beat, in corners, against walls or in the middle, following a 12-bar score. One bar = 4 beats.

Burst

Each burst first shivers as a disk of fog, then a hill of water is let go. Steep = how sharp its edge is. Rough = lumps on the hill that fold the light into a web of cells. Fog = how strongly it shivers; Fog time = how long before the hill drops (0 = at once). A tap makes a smaller, simpler burst.

Water

Clear = the water's thickness right after a burst: it wipes out the fog so the web snaps sharp. Fade = how long until the waves die down. Walls bounce waves back from the edges of the tank.

Light

Each particle is a ray of light. Bend = how far it travels after the water bends it; more makes thinner, tangled threads. Grain 0 lines the rays up in rows; 1 scatters them like film grain.
